## Strategic job
Continue the weekly curriculum after IZZY-W43-001. The first piece identifies the post-license problem; this piece addresses the next common failure point: believing the market ends after family and friends.

The Reel should move the viewer from scarcity thinking ("ya no tengo a quién hablarle") to a repeatable-market mindset. It should teach that the solution is not an endless contact list but learning repeatable prospecting channels and conversations.

## Audience
Primary:
- Newly licensed insurance agents.
- Agents who have already approached much of their natural market and feel stuck.

## Objective
- Education
- Authority
- Engagement

## Hook
> ¿Ya hablaste con toda tu familia y tus amigos? Eso no significa que se acabó tu mercado.

Use the hook immediately. Do not spend the opening seconds introducing IZZY or explaining credentials.

## Core message
Your natural market is a starting point, not the whole business. Sustainable production requires learning how to create conversations beyond family and friends through repeatable prospecting channels.

## Teaching structure
1. Recognition: "Ya hablé con mi familia y mis amigos. ¿Ahora con quién hablo?"
2. Reframe: The problem is not that the market disappeared; the agent has not yet built a repeatable prospecting system.
3. Teach three channel families without promising results:
   - Referrals and introductions.
   - Community/relationship prospecting and strategic alliances.
   - Educational/digital content that starts conversations and captures interest.
4. Operating principle: choose channels, track activity, improve the conversation, repeat.
5. CTA: invite demand for a practical follow-up guide.

## CTA
> Comenta MERCADO si quieres que desarrolle una guía con formas prácticas de abrir nuevos mercados.

The CTA is engagement/validation, not a promise that commenting automatically enrolls the viewer, produces leads, or guarantees sales.

## Moisés voice direction
- Conversational, experienced, practical.
- Use direct questions.
- Sound like guidance from someone who has built sales organizations, not like a motivational influencer.
- Avoid jargon where a simple phrase works.
- Do not attack agencies, uplines, carriers, or other agents.

## Brand / visual direction
- IZZY premium visual system: navy/gold, clean hierarchy, strong readable captions.
- Vertical 9:16.
- Keep important text and faces inside established Reel safe zones.
- Use captions for the spoken message; emphasize only key phrases, not every word with effects.
- B-roll should support the idea of expanding conversations/markets, not imply wealth or guaranteed success.

## Compliance guardrails
- Educational content; no individualized insurance recommendation.
- No earnings, lead-volume, sales, recruiting-income, or production guarantees.
- No claim that a particular prospecting channel guarantees appointments or clients.
- No carrier/product performance claims.
- Do not present unverified statistics.

## Success hypothesis
If the hook matches a real post-license frustration and the Reel gives a concrete reframe plus three channel families, qualified viewers may respond to the keyword `MERCADO`, giving IZZY an observed engagement signal for a follow-up content asset.

This is a hypothesis, not an observed Pattern Library result.
