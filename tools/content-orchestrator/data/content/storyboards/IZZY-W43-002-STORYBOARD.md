## Audio direction
- Primary voice: Moisés-style conversational delivery; confident, practical, not theatrical.
- Music, if used, stays secondary to speech.
- Avoid dramatic “get rich” or hype-style audio cues.

## Caption direction
- Burned-in/readable captions or engine-native caption layer.
- Keep caption blocks within established vertical safe zones.
- Prioritize semantic phrases; do not overload the frame.
